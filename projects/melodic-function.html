Overview

A rule-based music puzzle where mathematical functions become melodies players restore through interactive reasoning. The project brings musical patterns, mathematical curves, and a hand-drawn visual language into the same puzzle system.

My contribution

  • Created the system and visual design over ten weeks.
  • Explored the relationship between mathematical functions and musical patterns.

Reasoning through sound and shape

Functions provide a visual structure for musical information. The player works with that structure to understand and restore a melody; the design boards below document the original project.
