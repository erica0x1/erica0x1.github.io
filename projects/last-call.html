Overview

A cooperative tabletop game about running a busy bar. Players take on the bartender, server, bouncer, and manager roles, coordinating actions while balancing revenue, customer intoxication, chaos, and safety. An online adaptation lets players try the game in their browser.

My contribution

  • Co-designed the cooperative tabletop experience as part of the four-person design team.
  • Coordinated weekly tasks, responsibilities, and project deliverables as producer.
  • Organized task tracking and supported communication across design, art, and playtesting.
  • Helped bring the team's design process and takeaways into the final presentation.

Profit has consequences

Serving another drink can help the team earn money, but it also creates future demands on their limited actions. The intended tension comes from making customer safety part of the same decisions that keep the bar running.

Coordination is part of the game

Players plan actions with different role abilities and resolve them in sequence. An action can change the situation before a teammate gets to act, making communication and timing central to the experience.

Keeping the team in sync

A rule change affects the cards, rulebook, and next playtest. My production work focused on making responsibilities and updates visible so those pieces could move together.
