Overview

A short point-and-click story about memory, friendship, and words left unanswered. Explore a quiet hospital room and a sunlit swimming pool, examining letters, photographs, and objects to piece together a summer Haru cannot fully remember.

My contribution

  • Built an object-led investigation across the hospital room and swimming pool.
  • Connected inspectable clues with an evidence collection interface so players can revisit what they find.
  • Shaped the transition from clicking through evidence to a final tactile interaction.

A story told through what remains

Letters, photographs, and familiar objects invite the player to reconstruct a relationship. The two environments offer different emotional perspectives on the same summer, allowing the story to unfold through observation.

Teaching a different kind of interaction

Most of the game is played by clicking. When a later interaction asks the player to drag objects together, a brief contextual hint introduces the change without interrupting the scene.
