Overview

A spatial-logic puzzle that turns color perception into a deductive, perspective-driven game system. Players interpret how colored faces and structure relate, using observation as part of the puzzle-solving process.

My contribution

  • Designed and programmed the project over seven weeks.
  • Developed the relationship between color perception, spatial structure, and puzzle logic.

Color as a rule

The project explores how the same structure can be understood differently through color and perspective. The original design boards below document the inspirations, level design, and development process.
