Overview

A turn-based board game that exposes how beauty training becomes a system of resource pressure, comparison, and constrained agency. The physical game connects its theme with the materials and routines of play.

My contribution

  • Created the gameplay and visual design over twenty weeks.
  • Developed a physical game that connects resource choices with the pressures of beauty training.

Rules that carry the theme

The project's design uses the structure of a board game to make pressure and comparison tangible. The original project boards and complete ten-page rulebook are preserved here.
