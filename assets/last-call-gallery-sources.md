# Last Call gallery — November 6, 2026

Unmodified gameplay components from the team's online adaptation, https://yolanda699.github.io/Last-Call-Demo/ (repository: Yolanda699/Last-Call-Demo, main).

Sources under `img/`: `board.webp`, `mat_bar.webp`, `bar_cocktail.webp`, `bou_ride.webp`, `mat_bou.webp`, `ev_drive.webp`.

These show the shared game board, role powers and action planning, revenue/intoxication tradeoffs, safe rides, and a night event. They replace the earlier paper-prototype gallery. No standalone decorative artwork was added.
